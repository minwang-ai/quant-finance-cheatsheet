    Proofs¶

    goal: read, write and evaluate proofs. | Term | Definition | Example | |---|---|--| |proof| a method to find out the truth. | • Experiments /observations (physics)
    • Sampling (statistics)
    • Legal (judge/jury) etc| |A mathematical proof | a verifcation of a proposition by a chain of logical deductions from a base set of axioms. | | proposition | a statement that is either True or False. | • 2 + 3 = 5 (True)
    • 2 + 3 = 6 (False)
    • $∀n ∈ N, n^2 + n + 41$ is prime (False)| | predicate| a proposition except that it depends on variables (parametrized proposition, once we know what the variables are, it becomes proposition)|

    Combining Propositions¶

    construct new propositions by combining existing ones

    Term Denotation Definition to be true
    A and B A ∧ B both A and B are true
    A or B A ∨ B at least one of A or B is true
    (Note: this is inclusive or, meaning it’s also fine for A and B to both be true.)
    not A $\bar{A}$, $¬A$ A is false
    A XOR B no both, no neither
    (exclusive OR)
    A NAND B not And
    A implies B $A → B$ or $A ⇒ B$ if A then B
